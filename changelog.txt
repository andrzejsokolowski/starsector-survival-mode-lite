Version 1.2.1 (2026-11-19)
- Fixed a crash when opening this mod's LunaLib settings, caused by the % signs in the setting descriptions. They now say "percent".
- The retirement cycle and base delay cost settings now say that they only matter in a new game: the retirement date is picked when the sector is made. Turning retirement off still works at once.
- Hand-me-down freighter extra price default is now x1.13, because LunaLib's slider keeps two decimals.

Version 1.2.0 (2026-11-19)
- New: an on/off switch in LunaLib for every survival change, above its numbers. Off uses the vanilla value, for example no officer pay per battle, no corruption or no retirement.
- A changed sensor strength requirement for Analyze missions now applies at once, without a restart.

Version 1.1.1 (2026-11-19)
- Follows the survival changes in Starsector 0.98.5a-RC8. Lite stays halfway between vanilla and the new survival values.
- Officer pay per battle: 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 / 4 months of salary (was 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 8).
- Transverse Jump CR loss: x3 (was x5.5).
- Hand-me-down freighter extra price: x1.125 (was x1.5).
- New setting: Domain-grade parts back when you scuttle a ship, 62.5% (survival 50%, vanilla 75%).
- New settings kept at the survival value: Threat fleets drop twice the weapons and fighter LPCs, and the ship buy price per S-mod matches the sell value per S-mod.
- If you saved this mod's LunaLib settings before, the changed values keep your saved numbers. Change them in LunaLib to use the new ones.

Version 1.0.0 (2026-11-17)
- First release. Survival mode with every penalty halfway between vanilla and survival: prices, items for sale, loot, fuel, salaries, colony costs, officers for hire, story points, retirement and more.
- Optional LunaLib settings for every survival number, with its survival and vanilla value shown.
- Only campaigns started in Survival mode change.
