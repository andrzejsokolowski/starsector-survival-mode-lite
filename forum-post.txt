WHAT DOES THIS MOD DO?
Keeps everything the 0.98.5a Survival mode does, with every penalty halfway to vanilla. With LunaLib you set each number yourself.
[/b][/center]
[center]
[hr][center][size=12pt][b]In Between Vanilla and Survival[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Start a new game and pick [b]Survival[/b], as usual.[/li]
[li]Every survival number starts at [b]Lite[/b]: halfway between vanilla and survival.[/li]
[li]Ships cost x3 to x4.5 instead of x5 to x8, weapons x5.5 instead of x10, crew x5.5 instead of x10.[/li]
[li]Markets sell more, enemies drop more, fuel tanks hold more, colonies grow faster, 3 story points per level instead of 2.[/li]
[li]What survival makes easier stays: threats grow 3 times slower, more Domain-grade parts for sale, bigger custom production orders.[/li]
[li]Normal campaigns are not touched.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Fully Customizable (LunaLib, optional)[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li][b]139 settings[/b], one for every survival number, in 12 tabs: Prices, Pay, Markets, Loot, Fuel, Colonies, Officers, Experience, Hazards, Retirement, Survival help and Rules.[/li]
[li]Each setting shows its survival and its vanilla value, so you can pick anything in between, or past either end.[/li]
[li]Want survival prices but vanilla loot? Vanilla fuel but survival colonies? Set it.[/li]
[li]The survival rules that are settings can be switched off too, like the military market needing a commission.[/li]
[li]Changes apply at once, in a game in progress. A few apply only when a new sector is made, and their description says so.[/li]
[li]Without LunaLib the Lite values apply.[/li]
[/list]
