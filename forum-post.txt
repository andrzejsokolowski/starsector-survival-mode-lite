[hr][center][size=12pt][b]How It Works[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Start a new game and pick [b]Survival[/b], as usual.[/li]
[li]Everything Survival mode does stays, but every penalty is halfway between normal and survival.[/li]
[li]Normal campaigns are not touched.[/li]
[/list]

[list]
[li]The same halfway rule covers every other survival number: hull mod and fighter loot, cargo scans, storm damage, survey and colony start costs, commission pay and more.[/li]
[li]What helps you stays as in Survival mode: threats grow 3 times slower, more Domain-grade parts for sale, bigger custom production orders.[/li]
[li]The rules that are not numbers stay too, for example the military market needs a commission.[/li]
[/list]

[hr][center][size=12pt][b]Compatibility[/b][/size][/center][hr]
[list]
[li]Settings only. Safe to add to or remove from a game in progress.[/li]
[li]Some values, like how many derelicts can be recovered, are set when the sector is made.[/li]
[/list]
